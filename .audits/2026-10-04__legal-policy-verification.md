## Decisions needed from Arnav and Aarav

1. **Contact/address:** whether to provide a real service or mailing address, and what disclosure requirements apply to your actual operating locations. No registered address has been added.
2. **Deletion:** choose the complete cleanup scope, handling of shared records/uploads/backups/provider logs, legally required exceptions and a supported completion deadline. Implement and test the pipeline before promising a deadline.
3. **Location retention:** choose a necessity-based retention window and implement expiry, including what linked guardians and active friends may read from past updates.
4. **Liability:** decide whether to add a monetary cap and which limitations are appropriate. The revised Terms have no invented monetary cap; review the existing warranty, liability and indemnification language before release.
5. **Governing law and disputes:** decide whether to specify jurisdiction, courts/venue, arbitration or an informal-resolution process. None is selected in the revised public pages.
6. **Age and guardians:** decide how to enforce the 13+ eligibility rule and whether identity/authority or additional consent mechanisms are required for your audience. A pairing link does not establish legal compliance. The [FTC COPPA guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-coppa-frequently-asked-questions) distinguishes an age policy from the obligations that can arise when under-13 data is collected; legal applicability to Syncly has not been determined here.
7. **Deployment:** identify the live hosting project and publishing workflow, and confirm the deployed schema/provider practices before treating this source review as production verification.

## Implementation and verification gaps

- Account deletion needs an isolated populated-database test, foreign-key handling and storage cleanup. A successful API call alone does not establish all-data erasure.
- Guardian-location/history access, Ghost Mode access policies, pending requests after unlinking and approval links need isolated database verification. The reviewed policies are broader than the latest-pin UI.
- Provider regions, contracts, backups, logs and retention settings are outside the reviewed source and remain unverified.
- Notification delivery on closed/suspended mobile browsers is not implemented as a remote push service.
- The privacy migration and other app-review fixes already in this worktree are separate release dependencies; they were not applied by this task.
